Hisaab

Your money, at a glance

Hisaab shows you where your money goes — in pictures and colours, not rows of numbers. Open it, and within a few seconds you can see what today cost you and whether that's normal for you. Everything stays on your own phone or computer. No account, no sign-up, no internet needed.

1 Add an expense

Tap the big + button at the bottom. It takes about three seconds.

Tap 1
Type the amount
Tap 2
Pick a picture — food, travel, bills…
Tap 3
Press Add expense
  • Writing what it was for is optional — but it helps Hisaab learn.
  • Bought the same thing again? Tap one of the shortcut chips at the top and it fills itself in.
  • Forgot yesterday? Tap Yesterday or pick any date.

2 The home screen

Everything here answers one question: what happened to my money?

Spent todayThe big number. One glance is enough.
Calm / Steady / HeavyIs today normal for you? Compared with your own usual days.
The seven barsYour last seven days. The gold one is today.
Coloured stripWhere today's money went. Wider colour = more money.
Still to comeBills Hisaab expects later this month, like rent.

3 The coloured squares

Under “Where your money went” you'll see boxes of different sizes.

  • Bigger box = more money spent. That's the whole trick — no reading required.
  • Each colour is one kind of spending. Food is orange, travel is blue, and so on.
  • Tap any box to see that one thing in detail — how it's changed over six months, and what you actually buy.

4 Insights & History

  • Insights — a calendar where darker days cost more, your heaviest weekday, and plain-English notes like “weekends cost you 1.6× a weekday.”
  • History — every expense, newest first, grouped by day. Search for a word or an amount like ₹500.
  • Tap any expense to change it or delete it. Deleted by mistake? Press Undo.

5 Make it comfortable

Open Settings at the bottom right.

  • Text size — three sizes. The whole app grows, not just some words.
  • Theme — light, dark, or automatic.
  • Monthly budget — optional. Set it and Hisaab shows whether you're ahead or behind for the date.

6 Keep your records safe

  • Your expenses live only on this device. Nobody else can see them — not even us.
  • That also means: if you clear your browser data, they're gone.
  • So every so often, open Settings → Export as JSON and keep that file somewhere safe.
  • To bring it back later: Settings → Restore from a backup.
Nothing to sign up for. Hisaab never asks for your name, phone number, bank or card. It cannot spend your money or move it. It only remembers what you type in — and it keeps working with no internet at all.
